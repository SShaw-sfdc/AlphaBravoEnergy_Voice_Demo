# Alpha Bravo Energy — Battery System Warranty Guide

**Article Type:** Product Warranty  
**Data Category:** Warranty  
**Audience:** Customers  
**Applies To:** Alpha Bravo Energy High Capacity Battery, Low Capacity Battery

---

## Warranty Coverage

Alpha Bravo Energy Battery Systems are covered by a **10-year or 4,000 cycle limited warranty**, whichever comes first, from the date of installation.

**Cycle Definition:** One full charge and discharge of the battery from 100% to 0% state of charge (or equivalent partial cycles as tracked by the battery management system).

## Performance Guarantee

In addition to defect coverage, Alpha Bravo Energy guarantees the following minimum capacity retention:

| Year | Minimum Retained Capacity |
|---|---|
| End of Year 1 | 95% of rated capacity |
| End of Year 5 | 80% of rated capacity |
| End of Year 10 | 70% of rated capacity |

If your battery falls below these thresholds under normal usage, it may qualify for warranty service.

## Covered Failures

- Battery management system (BMS) failure
- Cell failure resulting in capacity below the Performance Guarantee thresholds
- Failure to charge or discharge
- Thermal management system failure
- Communication or monitoring module failure

## Common Symptoms That May Indicate a Warranty Issue

- Battery state of charge drops to 0% significantly faster than expected
- Battery will not charge past a certain percentage (e.g., caps at 60%)
- Repeated low-battery warnings despite recent full charge
- Battery management system reports cell imbalance errors
- Physical swelling or deformation of the battery enclosure (safety issue — do not touch; contact support immediately)

**Important:** Physical swelling is a safety concern. If observed, do not attempt to move or operate the battery. Contact Alpha Bravo Energy Support immediately at 1-800-ABE-NRGY.

## Exclusions Specific to Battery Systems

- Capacity degradation within published performance guarantee thresholds (normal aging)
- Damage from deep discharge below the minimum state of charge threshold (below 5% SoC repeatedly)
- Damage from charging at temperatures below 0°C (32°F) or above 45°C (113°F)
- Failure caused by connection to non-compatible inverters or charge controllers
- Physical damage from installation errors or external impacts

## Replacement Policy

- **Years 1–5:** Full unit replacement, labor covered
- **Years 6–10:** Module-level replacement where applicable; labor at customer expense

## Initiating a Battery Warranty Claim

1. Provide your **order number** and **battery serial number** (on the front panel label)
2. Share your battery management system performance log if available (downloadable from the Alpha Bravo Energy app)
3. Describe the symptoms and approximate date they began

A Service Representative will review your performance data and determine warranty eligibility within **5–7 business days**.
