# Alpha Bravo Energy — Power Inverter Warranty Guide

**Article Type:** Product Warranty  
**Data Category:** Warranty  
**Audience:** Customers  
**Applies To:** Alpha Bravo Energy Power Inverter (all models)

---

## Warranty Coverage

Alpha Bravo Energy Power Inverters are covered by a **5-year limited warranty** from the date of installation (or date of purchase if installation date cannot be verified).

## Covered Failures

The following are covered under the Power Inverter warranty:

- **Complete inverter failure** — unit stops converting DC to AC power
- **Partial output failure** — unit produces power below 90% of rated output
- **Display or communication failure** — monitoring interface stops reporting data
- **Fan or cooling system failure** — internal cooling components fail under normal ambient conditions
- **Premature efficiency degradation** — conversion efficiency drops below published specifications within the warranty period

## Common Symptoms That May Indicate a Warranty Issue

- Inverter displays a persistent fault code (e.g., FAULT 03, FAULT 07, FAULT 12)
- Unit shuts down unexpectedly and does not restart
- Output power reading is significantly lower than expected for available solar/wind input
- Unit produces unusual noise (buzzing, clicking, or humming not present at installation)
- Status indicator lights show red or amber continuously

**Note:** These symptoms require diagnosis by a Alpha Bravo Energy Service Representative before a warranty determination can be made. Please do not attempt self-repair.

## Exclusions Specific to Power Inverters

In addition to the general warranty exclusions, the following specifically apply to Power Inverters:

- Damage from grid voltage irregularities or utility-side power surges exceeding 10% of rated input voltage
- Failure caused by incorrect wire gauge or improper grounding during installation
- Damage from operating in ambient temperatures above 45°C (113°F) or below -25°C (-13°F)
- Failure attributable to connected battery systems outside Alpha Bravo Energy specifications

## Replacement Policy

If a Power Inverter is determined to be defective under warranty:

- **Years 1–2:** Alpha Bravo Energy will replace the unit and cover labor for removal and reinstallation
- **Years 3–5:** Alpha Bravo Energy will replace the unit; customer is responsible for installation labor costs

Replacement units carry the remainder of the original warranty term (not a new 5-year term).

## Initiating a Power Inverter Warranty Claim

Contact Alpha Bravo Energy Support to begin the process:

1. Provide your **order number** and **inverter serial number** (located on the side label)
2. Describe the fault symptoms and when they began
3. A Service Representative will schedule a remote diagnostic session or field visit

A warranty determination typically takes **3–5 business days** from initial contact.
