# Alpha Bravo Energy — General Warranty Policy

**Article Type:** Policy  
**Data Category:** Warranty  
**Audience:** Customers

---

## Overview

Alpha Bravo Energy stands behind every product we manufacture. All Alpha Bravo Energy products are covered by a limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship under normal use and service conditions.

## Warranty Duration by Product Category

| Product Category | Standard Warranty |
|---|---|
| Power Inverters | 5 years |
| Battery Systems (High & Low Capacity) | 10 years or 4,000 cycles, whichever comes first |
| Wind Turbines (Large & Small) | 5 years parts, 2 years labor |
| Energy Meters | 3 years |
| Mounting Systems | 10 years (structural) |
| Connector Cables & Accessories | 1 year |
| Starter Kits | Warranty applies per individual component |

## What Is Covered

Alpha Bravo Energy's warranty covers:

- Defects in materials or workmanship present at the time of manufacture
- Component failure under normal operating conditions
- Electrical faults not caused by external factors
- Premature degradation beyond published performance specifications

## What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Damage caused by improper installation not performed by a Alpha Bravo Energy Certified Installer
- Physical damage from accidents, misuse, abuse, or acts of nature (lightning, flooding, fire)
- Normal wear and tear
- Damage from operating outside specified voltage, temperature, or environmental ranges
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect performance
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers

## Warranty Registration

All Alpha Bravo Energy products must be registered within **30 days of installation** to activate the full warranty term. Products registered after 30 days receive a reduced warranty term of 1 year from the date of purchase.

Register at: alpha-bravo-energy.com/warranty-registration

## Transferability

Alpha Bravo Energy warranties are **transferable** to a new property owner if the product is permanently installed at a residential or commercial property. Transfer must be requested within 90 days of the ownership change.

## How to Initiate a Warranty Claim

To initiate a warranty claim, customers should contact Alpha Bravo Energy Support with:

1. Proof of purchase (order number or invoice)
2. Product serial number
3. Description of the defect or issue
4. Photos or documentation of the problem (if applicable)

A Alpha Bravo Energy Service Representative will assess eligibility and coordinate next steps, which may include remote diagnostics, a field service visit, or product replacement.

**Contact:** 1-800-ABE-NRGY | support@alpha-bravo-energy.com
