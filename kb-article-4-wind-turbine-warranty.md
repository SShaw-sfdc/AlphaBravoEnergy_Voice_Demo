# Alpha Bravo Energy — Wind Turbine Warranty Guide

**Article Type:** Product Warranty  
**Data Category:** Warranty  
**Audience:** Customers  
**Applies To:** Alpha Bravo Energy Large Turbine, Small Turbine, Wind Generator

---

## Warranty Coverage

Alpha Bravo Energy Wind Turbines are covered by a **5-year parts warranty and 2-year labor warranty** from the date of installation.

## Covered Failures

The following are covered under the Wind Turbine warranty:

- **Blade failure** — structural cracks, delamination, or breakage not caused by external impact
- **Generator failure** — rotor, stator, or bearing failure under normal wind load conditions
- **Nacelle / gearbox failure** — internal mechanical failure under rated wind speed conditions
- **Tower structural defect** — manufacturing defects in the tower structure (not foundation)
- **Control system failure** — yaw controller, pitch controller, or SCADA communication module
- **Brake system failure** — mechanical or electronic brake failure

## Common Symptoms That May Indicate a Warranty Issue

- Turbine shuts down and displays fault code (e.g., FAULT OVER-SPEED, FAULT VIBRATION)
- Power output is significantly below expected levels for given wind conditions
- Unusual vibration or noise from the nacelle
- Visible cracking or damage to blades not attributable to impact from debris
- Turbine fails to yaw (face into the wind) properly
- Generator overheating warnings

## Exclusions Specific to Wind Turbines

In addition to general warranty exclusions, the following specifically apply:

- Damage from wind speeds exceeding the turbine's rated maximum (site-specific; refer to product spec sheet)
- Blade damage caused by bird strikes, ice throw, or debris impact
- Corrosion damage in coastal or high-salt environments unless the coastal-rated model was purchased
- Foundation or tower anchor failures (installation contractor responsibility)
- Damage from operating in wind conditions outside the IEC turbulence class the unit was designed for

## Service Access Requirements

Wind turbine warranty service requires safe access to the turbine site. The customer is responsible for:

- Providing road or site access for service vehicles
- Ensuring the turbine can be safely de-energized and locked out
- Notifying Alpha Bravo Energy of any site access restrictions at least 5 business days before a scheduled service visit

## Replacement Policy

- **Years 1–2:** Alpha Bravo Energy covers parts and all labor (including crane costs if required)
- **Years 3–5:** Alpha Bravo Energy covers parts; customer is responsible for labor and access costs

## Initiating a Wind Turbine Warranty Claim

1. Provide your **order number** and **turbine serial number** (on the nacelle nameplate)
2. Describe the fault symptoms, fault codes displayed, and approximate date of onset
3. Provide recent SCADA or performance data if available

A Turbine Service Engineer will assess the claim within **5 business days** and coordinate an inspection if needed.
